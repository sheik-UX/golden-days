Brand Story & Culture

"Loyal to the good times." You don't always know you're in them until they're gone.

Golden Days is a reminder to slow down and enjoy what's right in front of you: good people, simple moments, and wherever the day takes you. Born along the Massachusetts coast, we make drinks that let the moment shine.

01
The Inspiration

Meet Bodhi — Chief Morale Officer
Nantasket, Late August

Bodhi is our golden retriever and the true beating heart of Golden Days. He doesn't check calendar notifications, track algorithms, or chase whatever trend is hot this month.

"All Bodhi cares about is an open beach, a tennis ball, and being with the people he loves."

We named Golden Days after that unhurried coastal attitude. It's an invitation to take a breath, drop the day's stress, and stay fiercely loyal to the good times and good people right in front of you.

02
Our Roots

Founded on the Deck at 7:42 PM
The Deck, 7:42pm

Golden Days started around a weathered wooden deck table in South Boston. Four friends looked at the table cluttered with seltzers and flavored malt drinks and asked a simple question:

"Why does every canned cocktail need to be so aggressive, fizzy, and full of fake sweetness?"

We wanted a drink that tasted like what we actually mixed at home: clean craft vodka, fresh-squeezed fruit juice, and zero artificial aftertaste. No aggressive carbonation that fills you with gas, just a smooth, natural drink you can enjoy all afternoon.

03
The Philosophy

Real Fruit Juice. Zero Bubbles. Year-Round.
South Boston & South Shore, MA

In the canned cocktail world, carbonation is often used as a shortcut to disguise cheap neutral grain spirit and synthetic flavorings. When your spirit is pure and your juice is real, there's no need to hide behind bubbles.

"Simple by design. Easy to enjoy. Life's better when you don't overthink it."

At 4.5% ABV and just 100 calories with zero added sugar, Golden Days is versatile and sessionable across all four Massachusetts seasons—from tailgates in the crisp autumn to backyard cookouts and winter gatherings with lifelong friends.

Crafted Locally for Massachusetts

Independent, local, and proudly made in Massachusetts. We believe in honest ingredients, transparent labeling, and keeping things simple so you can stay focused on the memories that matter.
